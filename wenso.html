Uniting Pakistan’s medical campuses through a single, seamless digital corridor.

Wenso is a dedicated web application crafted exclusively for medical college societies, councils, and students. Across Pakistan, valuable surgical bootcamps, clinical research symposiums, bioethics debates, and arts revivals were historically trapped behind closed WhatsApp groups and scattered campus notice boards. Wenso transforms medical student co-curriculars into a collaborative, borderless ecosystem. Society executives can effortlessly list workshops, embed flexible registration forms, verify attendees at the gate with instantaneous QR passes, and auto-render verifiable batch certificates. Simultaneously, students discover opportunities to hone skills, network with peers, and cultivate a verified clinical portfolio beyond the classroom.
