ModMedicalis

I founded ModMedicalis to change the boring way medical students study in Pakistan. Instead of plain textbook text, we brought illustration and mnemonics first.

I edited and designed every single page of our book "Mind Your Minors". Each page took creative challenges and 5 to 6 hours of design work to make complex medical topics visual and easy to remember.

Started in 2025, ModMedicalis has now expanded across 20+ medical colleges across Pakistan. We created our website to manage book orders seamlessly.
