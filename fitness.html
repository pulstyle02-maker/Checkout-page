Campus & College Guide

Smart options when stuck at university cafeterias

High Protein Best Pick

Bun Kebab (Double Anda / Shami)

Ask for no heavy sweetened mayonnaise. Gives ~22g protein for around 380 kcal.

Chai Hack (Save 120 kcal)

Doodh Patti → Sanda Chai with Half Sugar

Standard doodh patti has whole buffalo milk + 2 tbsp sugar (~190 kcal). Half-sugar tap chai is just ~70 kcal.

Craving Samosa?

1 Samosa + Chana Gravy + Salad

Have 1 samosa crushed in chana plate instead of 2 plain fried samosas. Much higher satiety & fiber.

Ramadan Smart Tracker

Prevent lethargy, muscle loss and dehydration

Sehri Formula (Slow Digesting)

3 Eggs + 1 Roti or Oatmeal + 1 Glass Dahi Lassi (salt or stevia) + 2 glasses water. Prevents thirst spikes.

Iftar Portion Control

1–2 Khajoor (Dates) + 1 Samosa or Fruit Chaat (no added syrup) + Chicken Tikka / Kebab. Delay dinner by 45 mins.
