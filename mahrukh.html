Histology By Night

I created "Histology by Night"—a series of handwritten histology notes crafted late into the night to simplify slide identification and medical concepts.

Medical students absolutely love them, and they have been selling in crazy numbers across medical colleges in Pakistan because they make histology simple, clear, and easy to review before exams.
